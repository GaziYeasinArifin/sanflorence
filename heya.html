heya

A daily home decor magazine for your phone. Discover furniture, lighting and rugs from brands in Japan, Europe and beyond, then build a room you can afford.

What you can do

  • Read today’s issue. Ten new pieces every day. Swipe through them and save the ones you’d live with.
  • Remix your room. Snap a photo of your room, pick what to replace and set a budget. heya reads the room and picks a list of real pieces you can buy today.
  • See it first. Make an AI preview of your own room with the new pieces in place.
  • Follow creators. Browse room lists from designers, renters and collectors, and save any list as your own.
  • Keep it on every phone. Sign in with Apple or Google to sync your lists, saved pieces and profile. Signing in is optional.

Your privacy

heya uses your Apple or Google sign-in only to create your account and sign you in. Room photos are read by Google Gemini and not kept. AI previews are saved to your account, and you can delete your account in the app at any time. The full details are in our Privacy Policy (Section 04 covers accounts) and Terms of Use.
